## Answers to your three questions

1. **Another cause of UNMEASURED?** Not in the Executor capture. The remaining risk is path- or literal-dependent setup in the probe copy between L0 and the Executor (alternative (a)). It is unmeasured because L0 stopped both runs first.
2. **Is the blanket LF→CRLF replace safe?** Yes, for this file:
   - The working plan has 1063 lines and 1062 CR-terminated lines. That is all CRLF with no trailing newline, which is `json.dump` text-mode output.
   - `json.dump` always escapes control characters, so a string can only hold the two bytes `\` `n`, never a raw LF or CR. No mixed endings are possible.
   - The pred file's bytes are never hashed, only parsed, so its EOL is irrelevant.
   - The value 688.5 is not at risk from float rounding: 570 + 25×2.53 + 40×1.38 lands on the double 688.4500000000000455, which rounds to 688.5.
3. **Does `git show` apply autocrlf or `.gitattributes`?** No. For blobs it "shows the plain contents" (https://git-scm.com/docs/git-show). EOL and smudge conversion happen only with `cat-file --filters` (https://git-scm.com/docs/git-cat-file). The LF in the blob was produced *at commit time*:
   - There is no `.gitattributes`, and `.git/config` has no autocrlf setting.
   - `C:\Program Files\Git\etc\gitconfig:11` sets `autocrlf = true`.
   - So any clone returns the same LF bytes, and the fix is deterministic.
   - Caveat: on a clone with `autocrlf=false`, the *working* plan would be LF, and the committed pred (keyed to the CRLF md5) would fail L0 on the real recipe too. That is a fragility of the pred-keys-raw-bytes design, not of this test.
