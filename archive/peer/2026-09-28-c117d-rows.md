## Answer

**Verdict: supported.** The four FAILs come from the script looking terminals up the wrong way, not from anything missing in the graph. One part of the claim is wrong, though: the log lines it cites for the `#644 → #376 t5763` link don't show that link at all.

**1. Strongest case against the claim: its evidence doesn't prove the draft's row.**
- The card cites `diag_c117d_rows.log:78,90,92` for "`#644` feeding `#376` t5763".
- Those three lines show `#2626` t2813, `#376` t5754 and `#376` t5763 with wire `w0`. In other words, all three terminals are unwired in the R1 graph, and `#644` does not appear on any of them.
- The graph agrees. It has `term_uid 5763 … wire_uid 0` and `term_uid 644 … wire_uid 3268`.
- The sinks on wire 3268 are `#29240`, `#12195`, `#1114`, `#10068`, `#3045`, `#2136`, `#2213` and two `#639` Diagram rows (`log:52,58,67`). `#376` is not among them.
- So "the draft's row facts stand" is backed by a mis-citation. The real source of the pairing is the saved/base column, `facts_c117_qrt.md:14` ("#376 frame index t5763 | w3268 | #644 = #637 i"). That source does hold: in `graph_l2b3_20260928.json`, `#644` is on w3268 while t5763 is already unwired there too. That supports the pairing without contradicting it, but it is a different piece of evidence from the one cited.

**2. The claim does hold for the FAILs themselves.**
- **`#644`:** the graph has it as a terminal row: `{"term_uid": 644, "is_source": true, "wire_uid": 3268, "owner_uid": 639, "owner_class": "Diagram"}`, and in `objs` as `{"uid": 644, "class": "Terminal", "owner": "Diagram"}`.
  - The script only looks rows up by owner (`diag_c117d_rows.py:102`, `by_owner.get(u)`, built from `owner_uid` at `:87`).
  - A terminal's own uid is never an owner, so that lookup can only return 0.
  - This is a category error in the lookup, which is exactly what the claim says.
- **`#637` / `#10170` / `#23041`:** a grep for `"owner_uid": (637|10170|23041),` over the graph returns 0 matches.
  - The graph records a loop's terminals under the loop's inner Diagram (for `#637` that is `#639`, as with `#644`) and on the loop's separate tunnel objects.
  - So "0 rows" is true of the graph. The gate expected something this graph never records under a loop node, so the error is in the gate, not the graph.
  - Note that for these three, the rows are not "listed under another key". There are simply none under the loop. The claim's wording fits `#644` only.
- **Web check:** in VI Scripting, the iteration and conditional terminals are reached through the WhileLoop's `LoopCounter` / `LoopEndRef` references ([NI forum 4344904](https://forums.ni.com/t5/LabVIEW/VI-Scripting-locate-loop-iteration-and-conditional-terminals/td-p/4344904), [NI forum 4267714](https://forums.ni.com/t5/LabVIEW/LV-Loop-scripting-issue/td-p/4267714)). They are not the loop's own terminal rows, which fits what the graph shows.
