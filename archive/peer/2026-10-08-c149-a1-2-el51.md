## 1. Strongest reason the claim is wrong
The claim matches items by counts only. The reader has no object identity for any item: `uid_route: "none: needs a Selection List[] 6349400 op (unbuilt…)"` (`errorlist_D1_ring_p4s06_20261008_174730_20261008_181243.json:98`). Nothing links "3 undirected tunnels, 1 source-less sink and 2 output-tunnel items are gone" to any of the 21 edits. Three things in the same record argue against "resolved":

- **A new type-mismatch item whose source type is void.** Item 6 reads: "The type of the source is void. The type of the sink is long [32-bit integer]" (json:322). The other mismatch, "1D array of Void → 1D array of cluster of 2 elements", was already in s04 and s05 (s04 json:701, s05 json:658). The void→I32 one is new in s06. A void-typed source is a wire whose source has no data type. That is what an undirected tunnel, or a sink-only wire, turns into once something forces a direction. It appeared in the same file where 3 undirected-tunnel items and 1 source-less-sink item vanished. That fits **a change of error class**, not a fix.
- **An op error that was put off and never resolved.** Op 21, `wire_indicators`, raised "target BROKEN after wiring – a source terminal was probably unwired" (`diag_c147_p4s06v18_scratch.log:990-994`). It was handed to sink gate SG, which checks only that the right terminals share wire 23807 (log:998). Nothing ever read whether wire 23807 is broken. The structural PASS (log:1038) says nothing about broken wires or types.
- **The prediction knowingly left out one item.** The pred flags `uncertain_base_newly_unwired_inputs: [[10757, ["array"]]]` (`plan_ring_p4_s06v18_pred.json:170-177`). The scratch confirms it: deleting and re-creating w23807 dropped three base sinks, namely 9519, `(10871, 10757)` and `(23798, 23508)` (log:1002). A fully counted prediction is input +1 (about 56 total), so the real gap is −5, not −4. One input item is unexplained.
