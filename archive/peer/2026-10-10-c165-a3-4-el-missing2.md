That makes this a bug inside the predictor, not something it was unable to see. The Error List shows only that the structure is consistent.   - Item 49: `A80 w_k4f` (`:1007-1013`).
  - The source is `attrib_c159_p5s02a.md:32,35`. It records that `w4337` and `w9636` lost their sources (#5204 outer 9631 and #10404 outer 24363), and that they come back when the tunnels' inner faces are wired.

It then kept items 4 and 49 standing because "uid named by no action" (`diag_c165_a3_3_facts.md:14-15`). Those are the same two actions acting on the same two tunnels.
