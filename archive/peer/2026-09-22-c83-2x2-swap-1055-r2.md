Its `Wire` read **returned error 1055** — "Object reference is invalid" ([NI KB kA00Z0000019L75SAE](https://knowledge.ni.com/KnowledgeArticleDetails?id=kA00Z0000019L75SAE)). The `wire: 0` beside it is the *error path's default*, not a measurement. That 0 is then promoted to a PASS gate at `:101`/`:112` ("the border terminal is BARE before the connect") and re-used as the post-call comparison at `:103`/`:114` ("went BARE -> NON-ZERO — wire 0"). **The entire "no wire was created" observable on the real pair is a failing property read.** It also appears at `:99` *before* `deleted wire w7506` at `:100` — so 1055 on this bed predates the deletion and predates the op edit.
